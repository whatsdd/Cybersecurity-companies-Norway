# Code of conduct

This project exists to make one thing easier: finding out who actually does cybersecurity work in
Norway, and who is selling somebody else's. Everything else is secondary to keeping the data honest.

## The standard

- **Be straightforward and be kind.** Assume good faith until it is clear you should not.
- **Disagreements about facts are welcome and should be settled with sources.** If two of us disagree,
  the one who produces a link wins, and if nobody can, the row says that it is unresolved.
- **No vendor bashing.** Listing a company is not an endorsement; removing or disparaging a competitor
  is not a contribution. Correct facts about your own company, and leave others' rows alone. A company
  that wants its own row removed gets it removed, without needing a reason.
- **No marketing.** "Market-leading", "best-in-class" and similar language does not belong in a
  description of services. Describe what a company does and cite where it says so.
- **Disclose your interests.** Working for, with or against a listed company is fine. Not saying so is
  not.
- **No personal attacks, harassment, discrimination or doxxing.** This project involves security
  people; publishing someone's personal information, home address or private contact details is
  unacceptable regardless of their role in a dispute.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages and any other space where you are representing
this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that break these rules, and may block accounts
that keep breaking them. If you think a decision was wrong, say so in the same place with your
reasoning: the record is public and it is fine for it to show that we disagreed.

To report a problem, open an issue or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled discreetly.
